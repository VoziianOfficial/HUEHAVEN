A minimal approach

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. Images, fonts and interface libraries are served with the website rather than loaded from external tracking services.

Your stored preference

The localStorage key huehaven-cookie-choice records your selection so the small consent panel does not reappear on every page. This value stays in your browser and is not used to identify you.

Temporary interface state

Menus, sliders and checklist selections run in the current page. They do not create a marketing profile. Your contact form details are submitted only when you press Send my enquiry.

Change your choice

Use Cookie settings in the footer to reopen the panel. You can also clear this website’s local storage through your browser settings. Essential only and Accept both keep optional tracking disabled.

Hosting services

Your hosting provider may process normal connection information, such as IP addresses and server logs, to deliver and protect the website. If this site is viewed through a sign-in-protected preview, the hosting service controls its own authentication storage.
