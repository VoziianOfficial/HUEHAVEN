Colour you can live with.

Think about daylight, furniture and the way the room connects to the rest of your home.

  • Test large samples in changing light
  • Discuss washable options for busy walls
  • Include ceilings and trim in the brief

A quieter kind of colour.

Look beyond the colour card. Wall texture and light can shift the mood of a finish.

  • Check samples in evening light
  • Ask about ventilation and drying
  • Plan where furniture will go

A finish for real life.

Cooking, moisture and frequent cleaning call for a carefully chosen paint system.

  • Identify splash and high-contact areas
  • Discuss cleaning and surface preparation
  • Ask when the room can be used again
