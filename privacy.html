Who we are

HUEHAVEN operates an independent painting discovery website. For privacy enquiries, contact .

Information you provide

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, service interest and project message. Please avoid including sensitive information. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and discuss suitable next steps.

Sharing your enquiry

We do not sell your personal information. Where an introduction to an independent provider is proposed, we will explain the intended recipient and seek your agreement before forwarding your enquiry.

Storage and security

Enquiries sent by email may be retained in the receiving mailbox for as long as needed to handle the conversation and meet applicable obligations. Access should be restricted to people handling enquiries. Contact us to ask about a specific enquiry or request deletion.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of your information. Any rights and exceptions depend on the laws that apply to your situation.

Website storage

We store your cookie preference locally in your browser. This website does not include analytics, advertising trackers or an account system. See the Cookie Policy for details.
